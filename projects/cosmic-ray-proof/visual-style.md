# GOLIDE illustrated-explainer style v1

## Identity

Scientific ink illustrations on warm off-white paper, using charcoal outlines,
restrained indigo shadows, electric cyan for energy, and signal red only for danger
or corrupted data. The look should feel hand-made but precise—not childish, glossy,
photorealistic, or cinematic.

## Motion language

- Draw-on reveals, paper cut transitions, arrows, wipes, zoom-throughs, and diagram
  transformations.
- A visual flip means a new idea: scale change, cutaway, location, state change,
  comparison, or causal link. Random camera movement does not count.
- Use parallax and controlled crops from base illustrations to reduce generation cost.
- Never display dense paragraphs. Labels should contain one to four words.

## Canvas

- Short masters: 1080×1920 with title-safe central 80%.
- Long masters: 1920×1080 with subject-safe central 75%.
- Generate base illustrations without embedded text; add labels programmatically.

## Audio identity

Calm, curious narrator. Sparse ticking/pulse under the hook, small electrical clicks
for bit changes, low paper swishes for transitions, and restrained ambient music.
Silence for 150–250 ms immediately before the key bit flip.
