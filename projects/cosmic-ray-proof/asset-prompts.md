# Base illustration prompts

Apply the shared visual specification to every asset: scientific ink illustration on
warm off-white paper; charcoal outlines; indigo shadow; cyan energy; red only for
corruption; precise hand-drawn shapes; no embedded text, watermark, photorealism,
3D gloss, cinematic lighting, or imitation of any named channel.

1. **bit-cell** — Macro cutaway of one semiconductor memory cell represented by a
   precise switch and charge node, isolated composition, room for programmatic 0/1.
2. **cause-icons** — Three separate ink icons: hooded attacker silhouette, software
   insect, cracked microchip; neutral infographic layout.
3. **scale-journey** — Nested cutaway linking desk computer, building, atmosphere,
   Earth and deep space along one continuous diagonal path.
4. **earth-cosmic-ray** — Earth cross-section with atmosphere layers and one cyan
   high-energy particle approaching from space.
5. **atmosphere-shower** — Scientific diagram of one primary particle collision
   branching into a cascade of secondary particles in the atmosphere.
6. **room-cross-section** — Human at desk in cutaway room while faint particle paths
   pass through matter; calm, not frightening.
7. **chip-cutaway** — Layered computer memory package opening into silicon cells,
   strong zoom path and clean technical geometry.
8. **charge-bloom** — Particle track through silicon producing a local cyan charge
   bloom at one sensitive node.
9. **healthy-chip-wrong-data** — Intact chip beside a grid of correct cyan cells with
   exactly one red data cell.
10. **memory-cup** — Two-panel visual metaphor: microscopic cup empty and filled with
    cyan charge, clean enough to morph into a transistor cell.
11. **swarm-satellite** — Accurate generic scientific illustration of an ESA Swarm-
    class magnetic-field satellite above Earth; avoid logos and false labels.
12. **swarm-constellation** — Three matching satellites in formation around Earth
    with subtle magnetic-field lines.
13. **earth-field-map** — Earth with magnetic field and highlighted South Atlantic
    region, designed for later accurate programmatic labels.
14. **ecc-diagram** — Data tiles surrounded by extra check tiles, one corrupted red
    tile, with clear paths for detect-and-correct animation.
15. **programmer-vs-physics** — Split composition: programmer writes a bit on left;
    physical particle changes stored charge on right.
16. **ecc-shield** — Abstract but scientific finale: error-correcting pattern forms a
    protective geometric shield around clean information bits.
