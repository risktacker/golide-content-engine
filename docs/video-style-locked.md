# GOLIDE Black video style: LOCKED 2026-10-05 (updated 2026-10-07)

Approved by Esiah at 17:02 Lusaka on 5 Oct 2026 after watching the illustration-style rebuild of "Why AI Data Centers Are Replacing Air With Liquid": "perfect… even more than the reference… lock everything in." Updates since then were all asked for by the owner and are dated below.

This is the standard for every Short and long video. Don't change any of it without the owner's say-so.

## Topics (7 Oct)
- The AI and tech story people are talking about this week: launches, viral demos, big company moves, what known tech leaders said or did.
- Explained so a curious non-technical person follows it and wants to comment. No jargon.
- No specialist or academic topics unless they're mainstream news.

## Scripts
- **Connected speech (6 Oct):** lines are breath-sized phrases, and a sentence may run across lines. A flow check rewrites choppy drafts once.
- **Long videos are lessons in chapters (6 Oct):**
  - An intro with a spoken roadmap, then 3–5 numbered chapters, each opening with a signpost and ending with a takeaway, then a close.
  - Chapter titles and shapes come from the topic, never a fixed template.
  - The script email and Studio show chapter headings, which are not read aloud.
- **Emphasis (7 Oct):** each line's emphasis is its key term: a name, a number or the key idea.
- **Follow call-out (7 Oct):**
  - One natural spoken sentence asking viewers to follow GOLIDE and come back, with a reason, never a plea.
  - Spoken as "follow us" or "follow the channel". The brand name is never said aloud in any script (a Bantu word for gold, easily misheard); the screen pill shows it.
  - Asked once at the end, after the value, followed by one short closing sentence (reference endings).
  - Fresh wording every video. Never "comment to receive", likes, shares, links or the bio.
  - A check rewrites the draft once if the call-out is missing, late, too long or asks for comments.

## Visuals
- Black theme only: a charcoal vignette, a faint blue-violet haze, and three depths of slowly drifting, twinkling stars.
- One animated line **illustration per idea**, not icons:
  - Shorts have 4–6 scenes; long videos have 8–14. A scene never crosses a chapter.
  - Each scene is one gpt-image-1-mini picture: thin white lines on black, exactly one gold element, no text.
  - The picture is traced into ordered pen strokes that draw on with a glowing pen light.
  - **Detail pass (7 Oct):** the picture asks for a few fine inner details, a ground line and one focal point, with no stray lines. Strokes are drawn as smooth curves, main contours slightly heavier than details, and the pen eases in and settles.
- **Scene changes never cut.** The drawing dissolves into points of light that land on the next drawing.
- **Chapter cards (long videos, 6 Oct):** a gold number and title at the start of each chapter. The chapter then stays as the small top tag.
- **Captions:**
  - The spoken words appear as they're said.
  - **Key terms light up gold, stay gold with a gold underline once said (7 Oct).**
  - The GOLIDE wordmark, and the logo at the end.
- **Safe layout per format (7 Oct, after the Muse short ran off the sides):**
  - 9:16: drawings stay in the middle 68% of the width (tall phones crop Shorts at the sides), between 17% and 58% of the height. The tag and follow pill sit above at 11.5%, captions start at 63.5% and wrap at 72% of the width, and the wordmark is inset 12%.
  - 16:9: drawings sit between 19% and 76% of the height, below the chapter card, with captions at 86%.
  - The image prompt asks for a compact, centred scene with empty margins and no edge-to-edge ground line.
  - A multi-word key term lights up only where it is said, not every repeat of its words.
- **Reference-style update (7 Oct, approved by the owner at 18:24):**
  - Ending: value first, then "So follow the channel, …" with a white **+ FOLLOW GOLIDE** pill under the captions (top in 16:9), then one closing line as it turns to **✓ MORE ON ITS WAY**.
  - Accent colour per scene by meaning: gold, rose, teal, ivory or copper; never the same three scenes running. Softer accent glow; closed shapes get a faint pencil-shaded fill.
  - Shorts 7–10 scenes, long videos 12–18, each a different kind of shot (wide scene, close-up, metaphor, before/after, network, large symbol, cross-section).
  - The first picture is drawn by the pen; later pictures fade in already drawn while the old one drifts and fades, then the accent lights up.
  - Action sounds, only when a line describes the action and timed to the word: bling, chime, phone ring, notification, door open/shut, car, computer blips, coin, typing, clock, camera. At least 3 s apart. A bling as an accent lights up; a bling and chime on the follow pill.
- **Follow pill:** superseded by the white call-out pill above.
- **Thumbnails and covers:** a black-theme line-art YouTube thumbnail. The Reel cover is the most fully drawn early frame.

## Sound
- **Voice:** the owner's own recording is the master timeline. It is never retimed. Shorts are 0:30–1:30; long videos are 3:30–5:00.
- **Speech-to-text:** gets "GOLIDE" (pronounced goh-LIDE) and the script's names as spelling hints.
- **Atmosphere:**
  - Four synthesised, licence-free beds, rotated in order across videos, each in a key picked per video:
    - drift (the approved 5 Oct bed)
    - pulse (a soft arpeggio)
    - glass (sparse bells)
    - tide (a slow minor progression)
  - Level −17.5 dB, about 10.5 dB under the voice. That's 1.5 dB lower than on 5 Oct, at the owner's request on 7 Oct.
  - The bed ducks slightly while he speaks.
- **Effects:** only a soft whoosh at a new scene and a gentle hit on the closing logo.
- **Loudness:** −16 LUFS, true peak at or below −1 dBTP.

## Script teaching style (locked 2026-10-09)
- Structure unchanged: open straight on the topic with a blunt claim (never "Imagine" or "What if"), then proof, pattern, cost, reframe, action, follow call-out, closing line.
- Narration, not points: every sentence joined to the last with a spoken connective; no colon lists, no noun piles.
- The Feynman way: ONE everyday picture (kitchen, cake, shop, bus) brought in within the first three sentences, used again at the catch, and returned to in the final sentence. Real names and facts in simple words; example before the name; every technical term explained the moment it appears.
- Reference script: production-queue/golide-short-2026-10-09.script-plan.json (the "bake the cake" short).

## Light transition and dot background (locked 2026-10-09, owner approved)
- Picture to picture: the old drawing breaks into points of light that travel in a soft arc and land as the next drawing, which forms as they arrive. On the way the points change colour from the current scene's accent to the next scene's accent. The first picture of a video is still drawn by the pen.
- Background: the charcoal vignette, haze and stars, plus a faint grey dot grid (one dot every 1/27 of the short side, about 20 levels above black, dimmer toward the edges), just strong enough to survive phone compression and barely noticed.
