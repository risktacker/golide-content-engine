# GOLIDE YouTube publishing standard

This standard treats metadata as packaging and classification. It cannot rescue a
weak idea, hook, or retention curve, but it must accurately frame every strong video.

## Titles

- Shorts target 38–60 characters; long videos target 45–70.
- Use a concrete subject plus one honest curiosity gap.
- No emoji or hashtag by default. Add an emoji only when it contributes meaning.
- Do not promise a consequence the evidence or video does not support.
- Produce three candidates before rendering; choose the final title after thumbnail QA.

## Descriptions

- Never paste the script as the description.
- Shorts: two useful sentences, important sources/attributions, then zero to three hashtags.
- Long form: two-sentence hook and summary, manual chapters, sources, asset/music
  attribution, then zero to three hashtags.
- The first visible lines must state the video's promise without keyword stuffing.

## Chapters

Long-form manual chapters start at `00:00`, contain at least three ascending entries,
and keep each chapter at least ten seconds long. Manual chapters override automatic
chapters. Shorts do not receive chapters.

## Tags and channel keywords

Tags support spelling correction and topic clarity; they are not the primary discovery
system. Use five to twelve focused tags: exact topic, named entities, a useful synonym,
a real common misspelling when applicable, and `GOLIDE`. Never borrow unrelated trends
or competitor names.

Channel keywords describe the stable channel promise. Current base set:

`GOLIDE, visual explainer, science explained, technology explained, curious facts,
true stories, paper animation`

## Upload decisions

- Upload as Private, complete copyright/ad-suitability checks and human QA, then schedule.
- Category: Education; type: concept overview when the field is available.
- Audience: not made for kids unless a specific production is genuinely directed at children.
- Original video and title/description language: English.
- Recording location stays blank unless the real filmed location is material to the story.
- Review altered/synthetic disclosure for every upload. Select Yes when generated or
  meaningfully altered content seems realistic. The stylized paper format does not by
  itself require Yes; realistic synthetic scenes, cloned voices, or AI-generated music do.
- Paid promotion remains off unless the specific video contains a sponsorship or endorsement.
- Add a related long-form video to a Short when it genuinely continues the same subject.
