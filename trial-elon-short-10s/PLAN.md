## Visual brainstorm

1. **“You’re Elon Musk,”**
   - Generic hoodie-wearing executive dwarfed by a launch rocket in a glass-tower canyon.
   - Executive reflected in a giant phone while satellites orbit overhead.
   - Executive arrives in a tiny kart beneath an enormous rocket.
   - **Selected:** Generic modern executive in plain clothes, framed against a comically oversized rocket and city towers. No likeness.

2. **“the world’s first trillionaire,”**
   - Character surfs an avalanche of hundred-dollar bundles.
   - Cash elevator rockets upward through a vault roof.
   - Character balances atop a skyscraper-sized stack while bills rain down.
   - **Selected:** A giant block of recognizable hundred-dollar bundles lifts the character above the street as bills flutter around him.

3A. **“and you’re losing the AI race”**
   - Literal branded kart race through a data-centre street.
   - Server racks become dominoes pointing toward a finish line.
   - AI chips sprint like mechanical horses.
   - **Selected:** Side-tracking kart race; the generic executive’s dark kart strains while an OpenAI kart accelerates ahead.

3B. **“to a company you co-founded:”**
   - Character winds up and launches a company kart that immediately escapes him.
   - Character opens a garage door and is flattened by the departing kart’s wind.
   - Character pushes a blank white startup kart onto a glowing track.
   - **Selected:** In a separate startup garage, the character gives a plain white kart a final push; it rockets away before its identity is revealed.

4. **“OpenAI.”**
   - Tilt up from spinning wheels to a giant real OpenAI logo.
   - OpenAI kart bursts through a paper wall.
   - OpenAI logo illuminates on a glass headquarters while the character recoils.
   - **Selected:** Low-angle wheel-to-logo reveal on an OpenAI kart beneath a large real-logo headquarters sign, with a sting and push-in.

5. **“Google and Anthropic are beating”**
   - Google and Anthropic karts cross a finish stripe together while the executive trails.
   - Two branded podium blocks rise above an empty third block.
   - Two branded data-centre doors slam shut ahead of the executive.
   - **Selected:** Google and Anthropic karts surge across a finish line side-by-side while the dark kart remains visibly behind.
