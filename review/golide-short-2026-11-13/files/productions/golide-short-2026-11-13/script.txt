That blue USB port may be lying...
Blue usually signals SuperSpeed, while black often points to USB 2.0.
So blue means faster, right?
Not ALWAYS!
USB 2.0 reaches 480 megabits per second; newer ports may advertise 5, 10, 20,
40, or 80 gigabits.
But color is only a clue...
The standards group recommends blue for USB 3.0 Standard-A,
but allows other ways to distinguish ports.
What should you trust?
Check the SS logo, then read the specification.
Charging's separate; color doesn't tell you wattage.
What about USB-C?
Its shape doesn't guarantee speed or power.
