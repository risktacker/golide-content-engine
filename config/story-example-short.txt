# SHORT METHOD EXAMPLE (owner direction 2026-11-13/14): a Short that works, youtube.com/shorts/8e4TYDVZTwk
# ("Imagine you're Mark Zuckerberg", 666K views in 3 days). Study HOW it connects: the viewer is put in one
# person's shoes, every step is caused by the one before (a need, the move, the new need), one turn, a payoff that
# names it. Never reuse its sentences.

Imagine you're Mark Zuckerberg, but you're losing the AI race. Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic are miles ahead, but they don't know your true power or your master plan. Every single day, 3.5 billion people open your apps. That's almost half of humanity. So you do what billionaires do. You buy the best. First, you pay $14 billion just to bring in Scale AI's founder, Alexander Wang. Then you spend over $2 billion to buy Manifold, an AI agent startup. Then you steal top scientists from OpenAI, Google, and Apple with pay packages worth hundreds of millions. You call it Meta Superintelligence Labs. Now you need GPUs, so you build Prometheus, a full gigawatt data center in Ohio. But one is not enough, so you build another data center in Louisiana called Hyperion. And you stop waiting for Nvidia. You design your own AI chips called MTIA, the Meta Training and Inference Accelerator. Now you need a lot of power and electricity, so you buy up to 6.6 gigawatts of nuclear power. Then your AI is crying for training data. But you don't have to worry. You own Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp, and Elon Musk annoys you, so you clone Twitter and call it Threads. You're already deep inside people's lives. You know their name and their face, where they work, their birthday, where they went last summer, what they posted in their story this morning, who they message, every single thing they ever liked. You know what they buy and sell on Marketplace, which groups they join, who their close friends are, what they talk about with their colleagues, what photos they send their family or their wife, late-night chats at 2:00 a.m. And even when they leave your apps, they don't really leave. A code runs in the background and tracks their internet activity and online shopping. You even run a dating service suggesting people to each other. And now, to get a verified badge, they send you a video selfie of their face. And the insane part? People gave you their whole lives, willingly. You know their lives better than they do. Then you partner with the US military and Palantir. Oh still not enough? Your Quest can track people's eyes and facial expressions. Now you need real world data. So you partner with Ray-Ban and build AR glasses that capture real world data from a camera on the glasses. You sold 7 million glasses last year. Now every like, every chat, every step, every glance becomes more data. Data you've been collecting since 2004, long before ChatGPT existed. So you can create a hyper personalized AI. Google knows what you search. OpenAI knows what you ask, but Meta knows who you are and it's already on your face. That's why you call it Facebook. Now you have won.
